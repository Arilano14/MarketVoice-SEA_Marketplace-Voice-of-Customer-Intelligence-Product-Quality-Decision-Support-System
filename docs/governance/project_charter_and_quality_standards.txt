================================================================================
MARKETVOICE SEA — PROJECT CHARTER & QUALITY STANDARDS
================================================================================
Scope: Business problem statement, project scope, deliverable milestones, and Definition of Done.
================================================================================

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
E-commerce customer reviews contain critical voice-of-customer signals regarding product defects,
logistics delays, and packaging failures. MarketVoice SEA provides an automated data intelligence
and decision support pipeline to transform raw reviews into prioritized quality improvement actions.

2. PROJECT DEFINITION OF DONE
- Data Integrity: Zero data loss and 100% referential integrity in data warehouse fact tables.
- Model Performance: Cross-validated Macro F1 exceeding baseline thresholds.
- Test Coverage: 100% pass rate across automated unit, integration, and regression suites.
- Security Hygiene: Zero exposed secrets and automated PII sanitization.
