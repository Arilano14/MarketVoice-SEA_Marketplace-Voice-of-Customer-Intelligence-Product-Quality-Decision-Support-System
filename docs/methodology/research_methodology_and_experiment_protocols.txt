Scope: Research design, hypothesis testing, experiment tracking, and model evaluation protocols.

1. RESEARCH DESIGN & HYPOTHESIS TESTING
- Statistical Framework: Two-Proportion Z-Test on low-rating dissatisfaction segments.
- Statistical Significance: Alpha = 0.05 threshold for overrepresentation detection.
- Cross-Validation: 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation for machine learning classifiers.

2. EVALUATION METRICS
- Classification: Accuracy, Macro Precision, Macro Recall, Macro F1, Weighted F1.
- Inter-Annotator Agreement: Cohen's Kappa, Krippendorff's Alpha on Gold Benchmark sample.
- Ranking Stability: Spearman Rank Correlation (rho) under Monte Carlo weight perturbation.
