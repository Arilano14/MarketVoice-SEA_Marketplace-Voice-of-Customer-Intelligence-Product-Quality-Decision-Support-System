================================================================================
MARKETVOICE SEA — SYSTEM & DATA ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATION
================================================================================
Scope: High-level microservice topology, data ingestion flow, partition design, and storage policies.
================================================================================

1. SYSTEM TOPOLOGY & COMPONENT OVERVIEW
MarketVoice SEA is composed of four integrated architectural tiers:
- Tier 1: Ingestion & Extraction Pipeline (Batch CSV intake, UTF-8 normalization, SHA-256 hashing).
- Tier 2: Analytical Data Warehouse (PostgreSQL Kimball star schema, conformed dimensions, additive marts).
- Tier 3: NLP & Decision Inference Engine (FastAPI microservice, TF-IDF classifiers, multi-criteria PRS).
- Tier 4: Workflow Orchestration & BI (n8n webhook triage, Human-in-the-Loop ledger, Power BI dashboards).

2. DATA STORAGE & PARTITION STRATEGY
- data/raw/: Immutable source landing zone.
- data/interim/: Gold benchmarks and synthetic evaluation fixtures.
- data/processed/: Parquet partitions organized by marketplace source and extraction date.
- data/metadata/: Persistent schema profiles and quality matrices.
