================================================================================
MARKETVOICE SEA — DIMENSIONAL WAREHOUSE MODEL SPECIFICATION
================================================================================
Scope: Kimball star schema, conformed dimensions, fact tables, and additive analytical marts.
================================================================================

1. SCHEMA: marketvoice_warehouse

2. CONFORMED DIMENSIONS
- dim_source: Marketplace data sources (SRC_SHOPEE, SRC_TOKOPEDIA).
- dim_channel: Customer acquisition channels.
- dim_category: Harmonized product categories (34 categories).
- dim_product: Product master dimension (3,664 products).
- dim_rating: Star rating metadata (1 to 5 stars).
- dim_issue: Product defect and quality taxonomy (7 categories).
- dim_priority_tier: Operational decision tiers (P1 to P4).

3. FACT TABLES & MARTS
- fact_review: 46,007 customer review transactions.
- fact_review_prediction: Machine learning sentiment and rating model inferences.
- fact_review_issue: 18,863 multi-label aspect defect occurrences.
- fact_decision_queue: 5,090 prioritized action items with calculated Priority Ranking Score.
