================================================================================
MARKETVOICE SEA — PRODUCT ISSUE TAXONOMY & ANNOTATION PROTOCOL
================================================================================
Scope: 7-category product quality issue taxonomy, aspect keywords, and Gold Benchmark annotation guidelines.
================================================================================

1. 7-CATEGORY FROZEN ISSUE TAXONOMY
1. FUNCTIONAL_DEFECT: Product does not power on, broken parts, software/hardware failure.
2. PACKAGING_DAMAGE: Crushed box, dented packaging, liquid leakage during transit.
3. MISSING_ITEMS: Incomplete parcel, missing cables, absent accessories.
4. LOGISTICS_DELAY: Shipping delays, courier transit bottlenecks.
5. WRONG_ITEM_SENT: Incorrect color, wrong size, mismatched product variant.
6. AUTHENTICITY_CONCERN: Suspected counterfeit, fake branding, unverified serial number.
7. POOR_CUSTOMER_SERVICE: Unresponsive seller, refused returns, hostile seller chat.

2. GOLD BENCHMARK ANNOTATION PROTOCOL
- 500 reviews sampled across Source A and Source B.
- Double-blind annotation protocol with Cohen's Kappa inter-rater agreement computation.
