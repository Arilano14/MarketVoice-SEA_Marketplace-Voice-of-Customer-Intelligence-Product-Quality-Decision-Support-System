1. PRIORITY RANKING SCORE (PRS) SPECIFICATION
- Mathematical Formula:
  PRS = 0.25 * Support_Score + 0.25 * Dissatisfaction_Ratio + 0.20 * Recurrence_Score +
        0.15 * Severity_Proxy + 0.15 * Classification_Confidence
- Operational Priority Tiers:
  * P1 Critical (Score >= 0.80): Immediate escalation / supplier halt.
  * P2 High Priority (0.60 <= Score < 0.80): QA batch audit & human review queue.
  * P3 Monitoring (0.40 <= Score < 0.60): Quality trend tracking.
  * P4 Informational (Score < 0.40): Routine monitoring.
